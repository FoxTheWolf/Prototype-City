# 10: O nome GRID DOWN e referências de identidade visual

**Para:** Gemini (pesquisa na web). **Volta em:** `docs/tarefas/retorno/10-nome-e-identidade.md`.
**Usado em:** a etapa de identidade visual (depois da web). O Claude lê o resultado e propõe os conceitos de logo.

--- COLE DAQUI ---

I'm making a small personal video game set in a fictional American city around 2008 (night, sodium streetlights, noir mood). The city's power and telecom company is a fictional cheap, outsourced utility called **GridLink**; the game is called **GRID DOWN**, and its logo will be the GridLink corporate logo vandalised (the word "LINK" crossed out and "DOWN" spray-painted over it).

Please research and answer in Markdown:

1. **Name check:** are there existing video games, software, films, TV shows, bands or registered trademarks called "Grid Down" or "GridLink" (also "Grid-Link", "GRIDLINK")? List each with year, type and how prominent it is. Say whether a small indie game called "GRID DOWN" would likely clash, and suggest 3 short subtitles that would set it apart (e.g. "GRID DOWN: Sodium").
2. **Utility branding around 2000–2010:** describe (do not copy) how American power and phone utilities of that era looked: logo shapes, typefaces, colour schemes, how logos appeared on trucks, substations, street cabinets, bills and uniforms. Give 8–10 examples as descriptions, with what makes each feel "corporate utility".
3. **Graffiti over corporate logos:** describe common styles of tags and stencils painted over signage in US cities in the 2000s (tools, letter shapes, colours, how much they cover), in words.
4. **Five original logo directions** for GridLink that would read well when drawn with ASCII characters on a dark screen (simple shapes, one or two colours, no thin details), each with a one-line description and the colours as hex.

Rules: no real brand should be copied; describe, don't reproduce. Keep it under 1,500 words.
